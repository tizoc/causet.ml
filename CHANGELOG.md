## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Component trees with distinct Actions, Effects, Events and Outputs, admission
  guards, synchronous deterministic scheduling, and message derivation through PPX.
- Routed service replies, request identities, timers, event readiness and portable
  blocking waits for application-owned driver loops.
- Automatic bug discovery through generated simulations, invariant checks,
  failure shrinking and exact reproduction, with transition harnesses,
  virtual time and simulation across multiple nodes.
- Structured observers, execution statistics, reporting pipelines and projections
  of ordered logs.
- Runnable shop, wallet, P2P and projection examples, with tested guide snippets
  and traces shared across the three ports.
